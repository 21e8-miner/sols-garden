## 📖 About

**SOL'S GARDEN** is a serene yet fast-paced arcade game of meadow reflexes.

The Sun-King presides over his idyllic landscape, holding a responsive snapping bloom in his hand.
- **Catch the Bees & Butterflies**: Tap anywhere on screen to snap the bloom toward the passing creatures to feed the light and build combos.
- **The Golden One**: The rare golden beetle brings fortune and massive points!
- **Beware the Wasps**: Wasps wilt the flower with a painful sting. 3 stings and the garden sleeps!

---

## 🕹️ Controls (Mobile & Desktop)

- **Tap / Click Anywhere**: Snap the bloom directly toward where you touch.
- **Spacebar / Enter (Desktop)**: Auto-snaps toward the nearest beneficial creature.
- **🔊 Sound Button / M Key**: Toggle synth sound on/off.

---

## 📱 Mobile-First Features

- **Portrait-Optimized Aspect Ratio**: High-resolution 720x1080 canvas beautifully proportioned for smartphone screens.
- **Direct Multi-Touch Response**: Fast, zero-lag tap response with tactile haptic vibration feedback.
- **On-Screen Mobile Audio Toggle**: Dedicated sound toggle for touch screens without physical keyboards.
- **Safe Area & Notch Support**: Full edge-to-edge layout with `viewport-fit=cover` and safe-area padding.
